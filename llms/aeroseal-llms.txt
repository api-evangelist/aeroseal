# Aeroseal

> Aeroseal is a building-envelope and duct air-sealing technology company headquartered in Miamisburg, Ohio. Its patented aerosol process injects a fog of non-toxic sealant particles into a pressurized duct system or building envelope, where the particles collect at the edges of leaks and seal them from the inside. Aeroseal sells computer-controlled sealing equipment and on-machine software to a dealer network under the HomeSeal Connect and AeroBarrier Connect product lines. It is an equipment, materials and contractor-software business, not a developer platform.

## APIs

Aeroseal publishes no public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable specification as of 2026-08-06. A live application backend exists at https://api.aeroseal.com (it answers `live (2.44)` at the root) but it serves the company's own connected sealing equipment and dealer software; it returns HTTP 404 on every documented discovery path and exposes no anonymous contract.
